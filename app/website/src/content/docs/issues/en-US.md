An Issue is a piece of background work that you hand to an Agent. It has a title, a description, and one Agent that does the work. Every Issue has its own conversation: the Agent's replies, its questions, and the Background Agent Jobs and Workflows that it starts appear there, as they do in Chat.

## Open Work

Select **Work** in the Pod sidebar. The tab is your inbox for background work: its badge counts the Issues that need your answer or wait for your review, and it lists your open Issues by where they stand, starting with those. **Board** opens the Work page. The page shows your Issues as a board or a list, with the scheduled tasks below them. When your Issues carry labels, select a label above the board to show only its Issues.

Work covers the conversations in your Chat list. A comment thread on a Page and a conversation in a chat application stay out of Work, so you follow their runs and schedules in those conversations.

The board has five columns:

- **To do**: the Agent has not received the request.
- **In progress**: the Agent is working, or one of its background runs is live.
- **Needs you**: the Agent asked a question or waits for your approval.
- **In review**: the Agent has stopped, and the result waits for you.
- **Done**: you accepted the result.

**In progress** and **Needs you** follow what the Agent is really doing, so a card moves between them by itself. A card shows the Agent's live runs with their steps and running time, and **Stop** stops a run.

## Create an Issue

1. Select **New issue**.
2. Enter a title and describe what should be done.
3. Optionally add labels, separated with commas.
4. Choose the Agent: your personal assistant or an AI colleague.
5. Choose **Start now** or **Start later**, then select **Create issue**.

With **Start now**, the Agent receives the title and the description as your first message in the Issue's conversation and starts working. With **Start later**, the Issue waits in **To do**. You can still edit its description, and it starts when you select **Start** or drag its card to **In progress**.

## Repeat an Issue on a schedule

In **Repeat**, choose **Repeat on a schedule** and set when the work runs, such as every Monday at 9:00. Each run sends the Issue's request to the Agent, and the Agent's result arrives in the Issue's conversation, so the Issue moves to **In review** after every run, even after you accepted the previous one. With **Start now**, the first run happens at once; with **Start later**, it waits for the first scheduled time.

The Issue's card and panel show the next run. Pause, resume, or run the Issue now from its panel or from **Scheduled** below the board. You can also ask the Agent in the Issue's conversation to change the schedule.

## Break an Issue into sub-issues

Open an Issue and select **New sub-issue** in its panel. A sub-issue is an Issue of its own, with its own Agent and conversation; its card names the Issue it belongs to, and the parent's card counts its sub-issues. When you accept a sub-issue, the parent's Agent receives the sub-issue's result in the parent's conversation, as a message from you, and continues the parent's work.

## Share the work with a Squad

When the Agent is your personal assistant, choose AI colleagues under **Squad**. The request names them, and your assistant shares the work among them. While a colleague works on its part, the Issue stays in **In progress**.

## Follow and accept the work

Select a card to open it beside the board. A card in **To do** opens the Issue's title, its Agent, and the request, which you can still edit. Any other card opens the Issue's conversation, where you add detail, answer a question, steer a run, or stop it. The line under the panel's header shows the Issue's labels: add one with **Add label**, or remove one with its ×.

When the Agent has stopped, the Issue moves to **In review**. Select **Mark done**, or drag the card to **Done**, to accept the result. **Reopen** returns an accepted Issue to **In review**, and an Issue that you closed before it started returns to **To do** with its request. When the Agent starts new background work in the conversation of an accepted Issue, the Issue reopens by itself.

## Who sees an Issue

The people in the Issue's conversation see it. Invite a teammate to the conversation to share the Issue. An Issue for your personal assistant is private to you.

While you are the only person in the conversation, the Agent answers every message that you write there. After a teammate joins, your messages still go to the Issue's Agent unless you choose otherwise. To write to your teammates and leave it to the Agent whether to answer, change the recipient below the message box to **Agents allowed to join in**.

## Background work from Chat

A conversation in your Chat list becomes an Issue when an Agent starts its first Background Agent Job or Workflow there, whether it is a conversation with your personal assistant, a direct conversation with an AI colleague, or a group. That work appears on the board without any step from you and stays there until you accept it. A new Background Agent Job in the same conversation reopens it.

## Scheduled tasks

Below the board, **Scheduled** lists the recurring tasks and pending check-backs that Agents keep in the conversations of your Chat list, with the time of the next run. Select a row to open the conversation that manages it. A recurring task that runs on your authority, such as a repeating Issue, has buttons to pause or resume it, to run it now, and to remove it. To create or change any other schedule, ask the Agent in its conversation.
