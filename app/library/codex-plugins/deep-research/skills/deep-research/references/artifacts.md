# Working artifacts

`report/report.md` is the only required artifact and the complete user-facing result. Any plan, note, JSON file, evidence index, source archive, or bundle is optional working state. The report must remain understandable and complete if all working artifacts are removed.

Web and browser tools may archive observations automatically. When a durable copy of data-source or internal material helps the research, use `archive_source.ts` and keep its returned hash rather than recomputing one. Archiving is optional and does not replace a clear citation in the report.

Never store credentials in any artifact. Do not package or expose working artifacts as deliverables.
