# General research mode

Answer the task without forecast-specific arithmetic. Use as much or as little collection as the question warrants; there is no source-count target. A single authoritative source may be enough, while a broad comparison may require wider coverage. State material coverage limits and preserve a legitimate “not established” result when the evidence does not support a stronger answer.

Put the complete answer in `report/report.md`. Optional working files must not carry any conclusion or qualification that the report omits.
